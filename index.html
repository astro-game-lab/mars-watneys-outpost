🚀 Mars: Watney's Outpost

How the outpost works

⚡ PowerRuns everything. Solar Arrays only work in daylight, so bank power in Battery Banks for the night.
💧 WaterWater Extractors pull it out of the ground, using power. Habitats and Greenhouses use it, and so does the crew.
O₂ 🥔 Air and foodHabitats turn water and power into oxygen. Greenhouses grow food.
💰 CreditsScience Labs earn them. Spend them, with materials from Mines, on new buildings.

🌅 The Sun sets every sol, and solar power goes with it. Watch each resource's rate and time left in the HUD: a big stockpile means nothing if it's falling fast. If crew health reaches zero, the mission is over.
